Our Leadership

Experience and Integrity at the Helm.

Aws Shallal

GM, Memory Division

Aws brings more than 20 years of silicon architecture and design leadership spanning memory, high-speed SerDes, and network transport ASICs. Prior to ForwardEdge, Aws was Technical Director of ASIC Design at Rambus, where he architected the industry's JEDEC/MIPI I3C memory side-band bus, led a multi-site 400/800/1600G retimer program in TSMC 7nm supporting 25G NRZ and 56/112G PAM4 SerDes, and delivered a DDR4 Non-Volatile DIMM solution now deployed at one of the world's largest cloud computing companies. Earlier in his career, Aws held architecture and design leadership roles at Inphi, Cortina Systems, Intel, and Nortel Networks, where he built and shipped transport framer ASICs generating over $500M in revenue. He is an active contributor to JEDEC memory standards and holds numerous proprietary silicon disclosures. At ForwardEdge, Aws leads the Memory Division, setting engineering and product strategy for the company's next-generation memory silicon and IP.

Weifeng (Winston) Zhao

SVP of IC Operations, Memory Division

Weifeng (Winston) Zhao is Senior Vice President of IC Operations for the Memory Division at ForwardEdge ASIC, a Lockheed Martin company. He brings more than 20 years of semiconductor industry experience in foundry engineering, product/test engineering, reliability, manufacturing, and supply chain management. Winston leads global operations for DDR5 and DDR6 memory-interface products, driving product development, qualification, production ramp, yield, quality, and supply continuity. Previously, he held leadership positions at Rambus, Aquantia (now part of Marvell), Samsung, and PDF Solutions, with a strong track record of building high performing engineering teams and bringing advanced semiconductor products to high volume production. He holds a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and has contributed to more than 20 publications, conference presentations, and patents.

Brad Chung

VP, Worldwide Marketing & Sales

Brad Chung is VP, Worldwide Marketing & Sales at ForwardEdge ASIC's Memory Division. Starting his career at Samsung Electronics and continuing through leadership roles at global technology companies including ICS (Integrated Circuit Systems), IDT, Inphi, and Rambus, Brad has specialized in promoting and selling high-performance semiconductor products — particularly SSTL bus interfaces — to major DRAM makers across DDR1 through DDR4 generations. At ForwardEdge, Brad is engaging in JEDEC activities as part of his role as VP, Worldwide Sales and Marketing, bringing a passionate and proven track record as a sales leader in the semiconductor industry.

Satheesh Kumar Dhanabalan

Director, New Products, Memory Division

At ForwardEdge ASIC, Satheesh leads new product development (NPI) for memory interface IC programs, along with foundry operations and customer engagement. Prior to ForwardEdge ASIC, he held leadership roles at Rambus, GlobalFoundries, and Micron Technology, with a proven track record of driving multiple programs from design through qualification and mass production, leading cross-functional teams and executing successful process transfers. His experience spans technical program management, design enablement, NAND device and process integration, customer interface, and yield engineering — working with leading foundries including TSMC and GlobalFoundries. Satheesh holds an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from the National University of Singapore and an Advanced Certificate in Applied AI & Machine Learning from IIT Madras.
